## Proposed design (needs Simon)
- **New order:** Förvaltaren › SBS › Heimstaden › Wallenstam (after 1 Dec) › HomeQ (only if POST is allowed). Stena is out.
- **Förvaltaren:** same model as SSSB. One JSON request per run, plus detail pages chosen by public facts (≤ 10/run) for top points. Winnable = your points > top points, tied = equal. Points are kept in a secret (`FV_POINTS` + `FV_POINTS_ON`, the date they were read; +1/day from then). Alerts tagged "FV ·". In scope: all of Sundbyberg (tier 2); 1 room only, and the same rent and move-in rules.
- **SBS, Heimstaden:** no competition figure, so no "winnable". Either a "new matching listing" alert (type, rent and window pass) or digest only. Today neither has anything at ≤ 6,000 in your areas, so either choice would stay quiet for now.
