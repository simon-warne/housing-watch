## Recommendation: where the database lives
**A separate `data` branch** holding the SQLite file, committed only when something changed. Durable, no expiry, history is visible. Artifacts expire after 90 days; the cache is evicted after 7 days unused (too fragile for six months of history). ~24 listings × a few changes a day keeps it small.
