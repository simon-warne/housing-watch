## Rules for every request
Same as M5 part A: GET only, logged out, the `USER_AGENT` from `sssb.py`, at least 5 s between requests, about 10 requests per site (about 50 in total). Every request goes into `docs/reports/evidence/M6-requests.log` (time, URL, status, size). If a site blocks bots (Cloudflare challenge, 403, captcha), its terms or robots.txt rule out automated reading, or it shows nothing without a login, I stop work on that site and note it. I won't try to get around a block.

## Per site, in order: SBS, HomeQ, Förvaltaren, Wallenstam, Heimstaden, Stena
1. **robots.txt and terms** (user terms, and anything about scraping or automated reading).
2. **Data source:** where the listings come from. Either a JSON/XHR endpoint the page calls (found in the page source and the JS bundle, without running a browser) or server-rendered HTML. Whether one request gives every listing or it needs paging. Whether Stockholm can be filtered.
3. **Fields visible logged out:** type, area or municipality, address, rooms, size, rent, move-in, deadline, contract type (student, youth, short-term), and requirements (income, age, student status).
4. **Allocation:** queue time, points, lottery or first come. What resets the queue (signing, declining). Any penalty for declining.
5. **Competition figure:** whether any figure is visible logged out (top queue time, number of applicants, your place in line). This decides whether "winnable" is possible at all.
6. **Overlap:** for Heimstaden and Stena directly, whether their listings are the same ones HomeQ shows (compare ids or addresses from the same day).
7. **Fixtures** in `tests/fixtures/<source>/`, dated, with a README (source, date, user-agent). Session tokens and cookies replaced, as for SSSB. Saved for list and detail pages only for sites that are still possible.
